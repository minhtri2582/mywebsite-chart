🌱 Additional “Culture Fit” Questions for SREs

6. Ownership & Accountability

Question:
Tell me about a time you took ownership of an issue even though it wasn’t directly your responsibility.

Answer:
- Situation: A monitoring alert came from a service owned by another team, and no one was on-call.
- Task: I could have ignored it, but I wanted to maintain system reliability.
- Action: I investigated logs, stabilized the service by restarting the failing pod, and then created a Jira ticket with detailed steps for the owning team.
- Result: The issue was resolved quickly, and leadership later praised the cross-team ownership culture.

⸻

7. Working Under Pressure

Question:
Give me an example of how you managed stress during a high-severity incident.

Answer:
- Situation: During Black Friday, our payment API had latency issues.
- Task: I needed to stay calm and lead incident response.
- Action: I split responsibilities — one person handled comms, one handled logs, I focused on rollback.
- Result: Incident resolved in 8 minutes with minimal business impact. Staying calm allowed others to stay focused.

⸻

8. Continuous Learning

Question:
What’s something new you learned recently that made you better as an SRE?

Answer:
- Situation: We struggled with debugging container network issues.
- Action: I took time to learn eBPF tracing tools.
- Result: Applied this knowledge to identify a DNS resolution bottleneck in Kubernetes, improving pod startup times by 20%.

⸻

9. Communication & Team Fit

Question:
Tell me about a time you had to explain a complex technical issue to a non-technical stakeholder.

Answer:
- Situation: Our CEO asked why an outage occurred and why recovery took 30 minutes.
- Action: I explained using simple analogies (like traffic congestion), avoiding jargon. I focused on customer impact and what improvements we were implementing.
- Result: The CEO appreciated the clarity and trusted our team’s plan.

⸻

10. Team Collaboration / Mentoring

Question:
Have you ever mentored a teammate or shared reliability knowledge with others?

Answer:
- Situation: A junior engineer was new to on-call duty.
- Action: I created a short runbook and shadowed them during their first incident.
- Result: They felt confident, and later contributed improvements to the runbook themselves.

⸻
